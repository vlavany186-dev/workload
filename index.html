How Raithu Work Connect Works

Designed for simplicity so rural workers can secure daily wage work without hassle

1

Search Nearby Work

Filter jobs by your village, mandal, or district. Select specific work like paddy transplanting, harvesting, weeding, or tractor plowing.

2

Know Wage & Timings

Check the fixed daily wage in Indian Rupees (₹), working hours, and whether the farmer provides free lunch, drinking water, or transport.

3

Direct Call & Confirmation

Click the green phone button to call the farmer directly. Agree on the work and arrive at the farm without middleman commission.
